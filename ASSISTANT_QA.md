# EasySoft POS — Owner Help & FAQ

Owner-facing how-to knowledge base for the in-app AI assistant. Each entry is a
question (English / Portuguese) followed by a short, practical answer. The
assistant retrieves the closest entries and answers in the user's language.

## What can this assistant do for me? / O que é que este assistente pode fazer por mim?
I answer questions about your own business — sales, products, stock, employees, customers, and profit — and I explain how to use the app. Ask me things like "how much did I sell today?", "which employee sold the most?", or "how do I add a product?". I'm read-only: I look things up and explain, but I never change your data.

## How do I log in? / Como faço login?
Open the app, enter your username or email and your password, then tap Sign in. On a till device you can log in faster with your employee number and PIN. Owners and managers usually log in from a browser or phone with username + password.

## I forgot my password — what do I do? / Esqueci-me da palavra-passe — o que faço?
Ask the business owner or an admin to reset it for you from the employee or user settings. For security, passwords can't be recovered — only reset. Once reset, you'll get a new password to sign in and can change it after.

## What's the difference between owner, admin, manager and cashier? / Qual a diferença entre proprietário, admin, gerente e caixa?
Owner has full access to everything. Admin can do everything except clear/delete data. Manager handles day-to-day work and reports but not employees, settings, or profit figures. Cashier works the till (sales) only. You set each person's role when you add them as an employee.

## Can I use the system on my phone or from home? / Posso usar o sistema no telemóvel ou a partir de casa?
Yes. Owners and managers can open the app in a browser or on a phone to check reports, stock, and sales remotely. The till itself (checkout + hardware) runs on the shop's till device. You'll see the management screens, not the cash register, when you log in from a phone.

## Does the system work without internet? / O sistema funciona sem internet?
Yes — the till is offline-first. Sales are saved on the device immediately and sync to the cloud automatically when the connection returns (about every few minutes). You can keep selling during an outage without losing anything.

## How do I switch the app language between English and Portuguese? / Como mudo o idioma entre inglês e português?
Use the language switcher (usually near the top of the screen or in Settings) to toggle between English and Português. The whole interface updates instantly. You can also just ask me in Portuguese and I'll reply in Portuguese.

## How do I change my business name or details? / Como altero o nome ou os dados do meu negócio?
Go to Settings and open your business/company details. There you can update the business name, tax number (NIF), address, and contact info that appear on receipts and invoices. Only owners/admins can change these.

## How do I make a sale? / Como faço uma venda?
Open POS (the till screen), tap the products the customer is buying to add them to the order on the right, then tap the payment button, choose cash or card, and confirm. The receipt prints and the sale is recorded. Stock and reports update automatically.

## How do I add items to a sale? / Como adiciono itens a uma venda?
On the POS screen, tap a product tile to add one to the cart; tap again to add more, or adjust the quantity in the order panel. You can browse by category to find items faster, or scan a barcode. Remove an item by tapping it in the order panel.

## How do I apply a discount to a sale? / Como aplico um desconto numa venda?
In the order panel, choose the discount option and enter either a percentage or a fixed amount — you can discount a single line or the whole order. The total updates immediately. Discounts can't exceed the order value.

## How do I take a cash payment and give change? / Como recebo em dinheiro e dou troco?
Tap Payment, choose Cash, and enter the amount the customer gave you — the app calculates the change to return. Confirm to open the drawer (if connected) and print the receipt. The sale is recorded as a cash payment.

## How do I take a card payment? / Como recebo pagamento com cartão?
Tap Payment, choose Card, and confirm the amount. Process the card on your card machine as usual, then confirm in the app to complete and print the receipt. (Direct card-terminal integration may not be enabled — the app records it as a card payment.)

## How do I split a payment between cash and card? / Como divido o pagamento entre dinheiro e cartão?
At payment, choose the mixed/split option and enter how much is paid by cash and how much by card. The app records it as a "mixed" payment and reflects both in your payment-method reports.

## How do I add a customer to a sale? / Como associo um cliente a uma venda?
Before or during checkout, use the customer option to search for and attach a customer (by name or NIF). This is needed when the customer wants an invoice with their tax number. If they're new, you can add them on the spot.

## What's the difference between dine-in and take-away? / Qual a diferença entre comer no local e take-away?
It's a tag on the order for how it's fulfilled — dine-in (eaten in) vs take-away (to go). Switch it on the POS screen before finishing the sale. It helps with kitchen routing and reporting; it doesn't change the price unless you've set different rules.

## How do I cancel a sale before finishing it? / Como cancelo uma venda antes de finalizar?
Just clear the current order on the POS screen (remove the items or use the clear/cancel option) before you take payment. Nothing is recorded until you complete payment, so an unfinished order leaves no trace.

## How do I scan a barcode to add a product? / Como leio um código de barras para adicionar um produto?
With a barcode scanner connected, scan the item while on the POS screen and it's added to the cart automatically — as long as the product has that barcode saved. You can also type or search if scanning isn't available.

## How do I sell an item that isn't in my product list? / Como vendo um artigo que não está na lista de produtos?
The cleanest way is to add it as a product first (even quickly) so stock and reports stay accurate. If you sell one-offs often, create a generic "Misc" product you can reprice at checkout. Keeping items in the catalog keeps your numbers correct.

## Can I put a sale on hold and come back to it? / Posso deixar uma venda em espera e voltar mais tarde?
This depends on your version; where available, use the hold/park option to save the current order and start a new one, then recall it later. If you don't see it, complete or clear the order instead.

## How do I add a new product? / Como adiciono um novo produto?
Open Products from the menu, tap Add/New, and fill in the name, price, and (recommended) the cost and tax rate. Assign it to a category and, if you track stock, set the stock and minimum level. Save and it appears on the till.

## How do I add a photo to a product? / Como adiciono uma foto a um produto?
Open Products, select the product, and use the image upload to pick a photo from your device. Good photos (like on Uber/Glovo) make the till faster and nicer. The image is optimized automatically for speed.

## How do I set a product's price? / Como defino o preço de um produto?
Edit the product in Products and enter the selling price (the price the customer pays). You can change it any time; it applies to future sales. Set the tax rate too so invoices are correct.

## How do I set a product's cost for profit tracking? / Como defino o custo de um produto para acompanhar o lucro?
In the product's details, fill in the Cost field (what you pay for it). The app uses price minus cost to calculate your profit and margins in reports. Without a cost, profit figures will be understated.

## How do I edit a product? / Como edito um produto?
Go to Products, tap the product, change any field (name, price, cost, stock, category, image), and save. Changes apply to future sales immediately.

## How do I delete or hide a product? / Como elimino ou oculto um produto?
In Products, open the item and deactivate/hide it so it stops showing on the till while keeping its sales history. Deleting is usually a soft action to protect your reports. Hiding is safer than deleting for items you might sell again.

## How do I organize products into categories? / Como organizo os produtos em categorias?
Create categories (e.g., Drinks, Food) under Categories, then assign each product to one. Categories group products on the till and power your "sales by category" reports. A product without a category falls under a default group.

## How do I set the tax (IVA) rate on a product? / Como defino a taxa de IVA de um produto?
Edit the product and choose its IVA rate (e.g., 23%, 13%, 6%). This is used to calculate tax on each sale and on invoices, so set it correctly for legal compliance in Portugal.

## How do I add a barcode to a product? / Como adiciono um código de barras a um produto?
Edit the product and enter its barcode number in the barcode field (or scan it into the field). After that, scanning the item at the till adds it automatically.

## How do I mark a product as out of stock? / Como marco um produto como esgotado?
If you track stock, set its stock to 0 and the app shows it as out of stock. You can also hide it from the till. Products at or below their minimum level appear in your low-stock list.

## Why should I fill in the product cost? / Porque devo preencher o custo do produto?
Because it's how the app knows your profit. With cost filled in, reports show gross profit, margins, and your true earnings per product. Without it, you only see revenue, not what you actually made.

## How do I create a category? / Como crio uma categoria?
Open Categories, tap Add, give it a name (and optionally a color/icon), and save. New products can then be assigned to it. Categories keep the till tidy and improve reports.

## How do I rename or recolor a category? / Como renomeio ou mudo a cor de uma categoria?
In Categories, open the category, change the name/color/icon, and save. Products stay assigned; only the label changes.

## Can I move a product to a different category? / Posso mover um produto para outra categoria?
Yes — edit the product and change its category, then save. Its sales history stays intact and future reports reflect the new category.

## How do I track stock for a product? / Como controlo o stock de um produto?
Turn on stock tracking for that product and set its current stock and minimum level. The app then deducts stock automatically on each sale and warns you when it runs low. You can leave tracking off for items you don't count.

## How do I set a low-stock alert level? / Como defino o nível de alerta de stock baixo?
In the product, set the Minimum stock value. When stock reaches or drops below it, the product appears in your low-stock list so you know to reorder.

## How do I see which products are running low? / Como vejo que produtos estão a acabar?
Check the Inventory screen, or just ask me "which products are low on stock?" — I'll list every product at or below its minimum level with its current quantity.

## How do I update stock when new items arrive? / Como atualizo o stock quando chega mercadoria?
Either edit each product's stock directly, or record a supplier purchase/delivery (Purchase Receipts), which adds the received quantities to stock. Recording purchases also keeps your costs and stock value accurate.

## How do I record a supplier purchase or delivery? / Como registo uma compra a fornecedor ou uma entrega?
Open Purchase Receipts and add the supplier document — you can even scan/upload the invoice and the app extracts the lines. Confirm the items and quantities to add them to stock. This keeps stock and cost data up to date.

## What is inventory value and where do I see it? / O que é o valor do inventário e onde o vejo?
It's the total money tied up in your stock — stock quantity times cost, across all products. Ask me "what's my total inventory value?" and I'll calculate it for you. It's useful for knowing how much capital is sitting on your shelves.

## Do I have to track stock for every product? / Tenho de controlar o stock de todos os produtos?
No. Turn stock tracking on only for items you want to count (e.g., bottled drinks). Leave it off for made-to-order items. You can mix both in the same shop.

## How do I add a customer? / Como adiciono um cliente?
Open Customers, tap Add, and enter their name and, if they want invoices, their tax number (NIF), plus optional phone and address. Save, and you can attach them to sales. You can also add a customer on the fly during checkout.

## How do I record a customer's tax number (NIF)? / Como registo o NIF de um cliente?
In the customer's details, enter their NIF in the tax-number field. It's validated and used when you issue an invoice with their tax details. The system prevents duplicate NIFs.

## How do I find a customer? / Como encontro um cliente?
In Customers, search by name, phone, or NIF. Open the result to see their details and history. During a sale you can search the same way to attach them.

## How do I edit or remove a customer? / Como edito ou removo um cliente?
Open the customer in Customers, change their details and save, or deactivate/remove them. Removal is usually a soft action so their past sales stay in your records.

## Can I see how much a customer has spent? / Posso ver quanto um cliente já gastou?
Yes — each customer record keeps their total spent and number of purchases. Open the customer to see it. This helps you recognise your best customers.

## Why would I add customers? / Porque devo adicionar clientes?
To issue invoices with their NIF, to build a customer list, and to see who your regulars are. It's optional for cash sales but required when a customer wants a proper invoice.

## How do I add an employee? / Como adiciono um funcionário?
Open Employees, tap Add, and enter their name, role (cashier/manager/admin), and a login (employee number + PIN, or username/password). Choose exactly what they're allowed to access, then save. They can log in on the till right away.

## How do I set what an employee can and can't do? / Como defino o que um funcionário pode ou não fazer?
When adding or editing an employee, choose their role and the specific permissions (sales, inventory, reports, customers, etc.). Give staff only what they need — for example, a cashier gets sales but not reports or settings.

## How do I give an employee a login PIN? / Como dou um PIN de acesso a um funcionário?
In the employee's details, set their employee number and a PIN. They then log in on the till quickly with those. PINs are stored securely and can be changed anytime.

## How do I deactivate an employee who left? / Como desativo um funcionário que saiu?
Open the employee in Employees and set them to inactive (don't delete). They can no longer log in, but all their past sales stay in your reports for accurate history.

## How do I see how much each employee sold? / Como vejo quanto cada funcionário vendeu?
Open Reports and look at per-employee performance, or just ask me "which employee sold the most this month?" — I'll show each person's totals for any period. Every sale is attributed to the employee who made it.

## What permissions can I control per employee? / Que permissões posso controlar por funcionário?
You can control access to sales, inventory, products, categories, customers, employees, reports, profit figures, transactions, orders, settings, and data clearing. Assign these individually so each person sees only what they should.

## How do I make someone a manager? / Como torno alguém gerente?
Edit the employee and set their role to Manager. Managers get reports and day-to-day tools but not employee management, settings, or profit figures — set them to Admin if they need more.

## How do I open the cash drawer? / Como abro a gaveta do dinheiro?
Use the cash drawer option on the till; the app kicks the drawer open through the receipt printer. For control, it asks you to choose a reason (make change, cash drop, etc.) so every opening is logged.

## Why does it ask for a reason when I open the drawer? / Porque pede um motivo quando abro a gaveta?
For accountability. Every non-sale drawer opening is recorded with a reason and a timestamp, so you can see exactly why and when the drawer was opened. This helps prevent and investigate cash discrepancies.

## How do I count the cash at the end of the day? / Como conto o dinheiro ao fim do dia?
Use the cash count / drawer close option and enter the amounts by note and coin; the app compares your count to what it expected and shows any difference. This gives you a clean end-of-shift reconciliation.

## How do I record a cash drop or float? / Como registo uma retirada de dinheiro ou um fundo de caixa?
Open the drawer and pick the matching reason — "add float" when you put a starting amount in, "cash drop" when you remove cash to the safe. Both are logged with the amount and time.

## How do I see how many times the drawer was opened? / Como vejo quantas vezes a gaveta foi aberta?
Check the Cash Drawer Audit screen — it lists every opening with the time, the employee, and the reason. This is your full audit trail for the register.

## What is a cash reconciliation? / O que é uma reconciliação de caixa?
It's comparing the cash you physically counted against what the system expected from sales and drawer activity. A match means the till balances; a difference flags a shortage or overage to investigate.

## Where do I see past sales? / Onde vejo as vendas anteriores?
Open Transactions to browse every completed sale with its date, total, employee, and payment method. You can filter and open any one for its line items. Or ask me to list recent transactions.

## How do I find a specific receipt? / Como encontro um recibo específico?
In Transactions, search by receipt/transaction number, date, or amount. Open the result to see the full detail and reprint if needed.

## How do I refund a sale? / Como faço o reembolso de uma venda?
For a fiscal sale, you issue a credit note (nota de crédito) against the original — this is the legally correct way to reverse it, rather than deleting. Open the transaction and use the credit-note option. The refund is recorded and stays traceable.

## What is a credit note (nota de crédito)? / O que é uma nota de crédito?
It's an official document that cancels or corrects a previous invoice/sale. In Portugal you can't just delete a fiscal sale — you issue a credit note that references it. The app creates it and keeps the audit chain intact.

## Can I delete a sale? / Posso apagar uma venda?
Not a finalized fiscal sale — that's not allowed by law. Instead you reverse it with a credit note (nota de crédito), which keeps a proper, auditable record. Unfinished orders (not yet paid) simply clear without a trace.

## How do I reprint a receipt? / Como reimprimo um recibo?
Open the sale in Transactions and choose reprint. A duplicate is printed (marked as a copy where required). Handy when a customer needs another copy.

## Where do I see my sales reports? / Onde vejo os meus relatórios de vendas?
Open Reports for daily and date-range sales, top products, and per-employee performance. For margins and profit, check Profit & Costs. Or just ask me — I can answer most report questions directly.

## How do I see today's sales? / Como vejo as vendas de hoje?
Open the Dashboard or Reports for today's totals, or simply ask me "how much did I sell today?" and I'll give you the revenue, number of sales, items, and profit.

## How do I compare sales between dates? / Como comparo vendas entre datas?
In Reports, pick a date range to see totals for that period; run two ranges to compare. Or ask me, e.g., "compare this week to last week" — I'll pull both.

## How do I see my best-selling products? / Como vejo os produtos mais vendidos?
Reports shows product performance by quantity and revenue, or ask me "what are my top products this month?" I'll rank them for any period you choose.

## How do I see my profit? / Como vejo o meu lucro?
Open Profit & Costs, which uses your product costs (and any operating costs you enter) to show gross and net profit and margins. Make sure product costs are filled in for accurate figures. You can also ask me for profit over a period.

## How do I export a report? / Como exporto um relatório?
In Reports, use the export/CSV option to download the data — useful for your accountant or a spreadsheet. Exports respect the date range you've selected.

## How do I see sales per employee? / Como vejo as vendas por funcionário?
Reports has a per-employee breakdown, or ask me "how much did each employee sell this month?" Every sale is tagged to the employee who rang it up.

## How do I see sales by payment method? / Como vejo as vendas por método de pagamento?
Ask me "break down sales by payment method" for cash vs card vs mixed, or check the reports. This helps you reconcile cash and card takings.

## How do I connect a receipt printer? / Como ligo uma impressora de recibos?
Use the Printer setup screen to detect and select your thermal printer (USB, network, or serial). Once selected, receipts print automatically at checkout. There's a test print to confirm it works.

## My printer isn't printing — what do I check? / A minha impressora não imprime — o que verifico?
Check it's powered on, has paper, and is connected (USB/network). Re-select it in Printer setup and run a test print. If it still fails, restart the printer and the till, and confirm no other app is holding the connection.

## How do I print to different stations (kitchen/bar)? / Como imprimo para estações diferentes (cozinha/bar)?
Set up printer routing rules so certain categories or items print to specific printers (e.g., food to the kitchen, drinks to the bar). Then each order's lines are sent to the right station automatically.

## Can I customize what's on the receipt? / Posso personalizar o que aparece no recibo?
Yes — your business name, tax number, and legally required fields appear automatically; you can adjust header/footer details in Settings. Fiscal elements (ATCUD, QR code) are added as required by law.

## How do I test my printer? / Como testo a minha impressora?
Open Printer setup and use the Test print button — it sends a sample receipt so you can confirm formatting, the drawer kick, and the connection before serving customers.

## What's the difference between a simplified invoice and a full invoice (fatura)? / Qual a diferença entre fatura simplificada e fatura?
A simplified invoice (Fatura Simplificada, FS) is for smaller amounts and doesn't require the customer's full details. A full invoice (Fatura, FT) includes the customer's name and NIF and is issued when they need it for accounting. The app supports both.

## How do I issue an invoice with a customer's NIF? / Como emito uma fatura com o NIF do cliente?
Attach the customer (with their NIF) to the sale before payment, then choose the full-invoice document type. The invoice prints with their tax details. Add the customer first in Customers if they're new.

## What is the ATCUD and QR code on my receipts? / O que são o ATCUD e o código QR nos recibos?
They're Portuguese fiscal requirements. The ATCUD is a unique document code and the QR code encodes the invoice data for the tax authority (AT). The app generates both automatically on qualifying documents.

## What is SAF-T and how do I export it? / O que é o SAF-T e como o exporto?
SAF-T (PT) is the standard tax file Portuguese businesses submit to AT. The app can export a SAF-T XML of your sales for a period. Find the export in the fiscal/reports area and send the file to your accountant or AT.

## Is this software certified by AT? / Este software é certificado pela AT?
Certification is the legal requirement for invoicing software in Portugal and is handled at deployment. Check your Settings/fiscal section for your certification status and number. If you're unsure, confirm with your provider before issuing official invoices.

## How do I correct an invoice? / Como corrijo uma fatura?
You don't edit a finalized invoice — you issue a credit note (nota de crédito) that references it, then a new correct invoice if needed. This keeps the fiscal record valid and auditable.

## What is a wholesaler invoice (fatura grossista)? / O que é uma fatura grossista?
It's an invoice for wholesale/business customers, typically with their full tax details and possibly different pricing. The app supports issuing wholesaler invoices alongside normal retail sales.

## How do I see delivery orders? / Como vejo os pedidos de entrega?
Open Orders to see delivery orders and their status. Depending on your setup, orders can come from the till or delivery platforms. Each order shows its items, type, and status.

## How does the kitchen display / order queue work? / Como funciona o ecrã de cozinha / fila de pedidos?
Orders can be shown on a kitchen/queue screen and routed to the right station, so staff see what to prepare and mark items done. Customers can be given numbered tickets to track their order. Availability depends on your configuration.

## How do I mark an order as ready? / Como marco um pedido como pronto?
On the order queue/kitchen screen, tap the order to update its status to preparing or ready. This updates any customer-facing status display.

## How do I change the currency symbol? / Como mudo o símbolo da moeda?
Go to Settings and set your currency (e.g., € euro). It then shows across the till, receipts, and reports. Amounts I report are in your configured currency.

## How do I back up my data? / Como faço cópia de segurança dos dados?
Your data is stored in the cloud (Supabase) and synced automatically, which acts as your backup. For your own copy, export reports/SAF-T periodically. Avoid relying only on a single till device.

## How do I add a second store or till? / Como adiciono uma segunda loja ou caixa?
The system supports multiple stores/tills under one business. Add a store in Settings and pair each till device to it. Each till syncs to the same account with its own device identity.

## How do I pair a new till device? / Como emparelho um novo dispositivo de caixa?
Generate a pairing code in the app (Devices) and enter it on the new till to link it to your business securely. Once paired, it syncs your products, prices, and settings.

## How do I change tax settings? / Como altero as definições de imposto?
Set default IVA rates and your fiscal details in Settings, and set each product's rate on the product itself. Correct tax settings are essential for legal invoices in Portugal.

## What kinds of questions can I ask you? / Que tipo de perguntas posso fazer-te?
Anything about your business numbers — sales, revenue, profit, top products, stock, employee performance, payment methods, specific transactions — and how-to questions about using the app. Try "how much did I sell this week?" or "how do I add an employee?".

## Can you change prices or delete sales for me? / Podes alterar preços ou apagar vendas por mim?
No — I'm strictly read-only. I can look up and explain anything, but I never change, create, or delete data. To change a price or reverse a sale, you do it in the app (I'll tell you where).

## Can you see other businesses' data? / Consegues ver dados de outros negócios?
No. I can only ever see your own business's data — it's locked to your account, so there's no way for me to show another business's numbers.

## What time period do you use when I say "today" or "this month"? / Que período usas quando digo "hoje" ou "este mês"?
I use your local calendar (Europe/Lisbon). "Today" is the current date; "this week" is Monday to today; "this month" is the 1st to today; "last month" is the previous whole month. You can always give exact dates too.

## Can I ask you in Portuguese? / Posso perguntar-te em português?
Sim — pergunte à vontade em português e eu respondo em português. I understand both English and Portuguese and reply in the language you use.

## Is my data safe when I ask you questions? / Os meus dados estão seguros quando te faço perguntas?
Yes. Personal details like customer names and tax numbers are hidden from the AI — it only sees anonymised labels, and real names are restored only in your answer. Everything is scoped to your business and sent securely.
