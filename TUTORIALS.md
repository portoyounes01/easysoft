## How do I add a new product? (with pictures) / Como adiciono um produto? (passo a passo)

**Step 1 — On the left menu, tap “Products”.**

![Step 1](/guide/add-product-01.png)

**Step 2 — Tap the “Add” button to start a new one.**

![Step 2](/guide/add-product-02.png)

**Step 3 — Tap this box and type the product name.**

![Step 3](/guide/add-product-03.png)

**Step 4 — Tap here and choose the category.**

![Step 4](/guide/add-product-04.png)

**Step 5 — Tap this box and type the price (a number).**

![Step 5](/guide/add-product-05.png)

**Step 6 — Tap this box and type the cost (a number).**

![Step 6](/guide/add-product-06.png)
